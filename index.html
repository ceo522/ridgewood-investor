Deal Structure

Flexible Acquisition — TIC Sale & Creative Financing Available

Not every deal has to look the same. The seller is open to creative structures that work for the right buyer. Don't let financing stop you from acquiring a running income machine.

🤝
Tenancy in Common (TIC)

Co-ownership structure. Acquire a fractional interest — split the cash flow, split the equity. Ideal for investors who want exposure without full acquisition.

Good for →
Passive investors, 1031 exchange buyers, equity partners

Seller Financing

Bypass the bank. Seller-held note with flexible terms. The property's own cash flow services the debt. Close faster, lower friction, custom amortization.

Good for →

Take over the existing financing. Keep the current rate, minimize cash at close, step in as operator from day one with immediate positive cash flow.

Good for →
Creative investors, low-cash buyers, experienced operators
